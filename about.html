Our History

LASS SKINCARE is a South African company that manufacture's and supplies natural skincare products through independent sales distributors. Our products are formulated using natural ingredients such as Tumeric, Lemon, Pomegranate and Honey. They are also infused with many vitamins such as Vitamin E and Vitamin C.

Our company has been operating since 2020, we are a member of CECOSA(Cosmetic export council of South Africa) and Proudly SA. Our products are SABS tested and approved through the support of SEDA (Small Enterprise Development Agency) and DTIC(Department of Trade, Industry and Competition).

We currently have over 60000 Lass Skincare business owners who have joined the business and are reselling our products in South Africa, Swaziland, Lesotho, Nambia and Mozambique.

Our Reasons

Our vision is to supply quality products at affordabale prices to all countries in the African continent. Most importantly, to contribute in sustaining livelihoods and providing income opportunities for all Lass Skincare independent business owners

Our Goals

Our mission and our goal is to help our clients and partners to achieve financial freedom and confidence through great skin
